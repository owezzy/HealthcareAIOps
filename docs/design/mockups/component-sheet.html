02 · Triage labels

Severity, tier and status never travel alone: each pairs an icon or code with a word, and each has a distinct shape as well as colour.

SeverityC-01
Critical High Medium Low

Distinct glyph per level (octagon, triangle, diamond, target). Never remove the label text — the shape and word survive colour blindness and greyscale print.

Neutral by design: tier is context, not urgency. Infra uses a dashed border because it sits outside the clinical tier ladder. Codes are monospace for scanability.

One horizontal rail, six fixed states. Completed steps show a check, the current step is outlined and labelled with aria-current="step", future steps are numbered.

04 · Evidence & AI provenance

Every AI statement is traceable. Evidence chips open the drawer; the AI header always names the model and prompt version.

Evidence chipC-06

Default, typed, and open states. The ID is mono and stable; the type suffix is optional where space is tight. Hover and focus use the accent — never a severity colour.

Confidence pillC-07
High confidence Medium confidence Low confidence

Three filled bars encode level redundantly with the word. High is green, medium amber, low neutral — low is deliberately quiet, not alarming.

Streaming stateC-08
AI findings streaming · 2 of 4 facts committed action withheld until evidence commits

While streaming, findings render as labelled skeletons. The recommendation card does not appear until the fact set is committed, so no action is ever offered against partial evidence.

Provenance headerC-09
AI findings ops-agent-3.1 · prompt v12 streamed 09:18

Model and prompt version are mandatory and monospace. “streamed” is the timestamp of the run, not the view — re-opening the incident does not reset it.

07 · Chrome

Two persistent controls in the top bar: the spend meter and the demo role switcher. Both fit at 30 px height so the bar stays quiet.

Budget meterC-17

Normal, warning and exhausted. At 100% the meter flips the console into Replay mode: agent tools stop, a persistent banner explains why, and reads stay available.

Role switcher · closed & openC-18

Each option states what it can and cannot do, so the switch is self-documenting. The selected role is marked with a check as well as its filled state; changing role never changes incident data, only permission gates.

08 · Light-theme parity

The Incident workspace ships in light. The same components resolve through the light token set with AA contrast; nothing is inverted or recoloured by hand.

Same components · data-theme="light"C-19
Critical High Medium Low T1Tier 1 Awaiting approval High confidence Risk · medium 12:40

Light borders do the work that glow does in dark. Severity text tokens darken for contrast on white, and the accent shifts to a deeper teal — the semantics never change between modes.

09 · Composition rules

How the parts combine. These are the constraints that keep the console trustworthy, not decorative preferences.

Facts before inferences

Facts sit above hypotheses in the AI panel, in solid rows with direct evidence. A hypothesis may never appear above the fact that contradicts it.

Actions stay human

The agent drafts and recommends; only a human with the Approver role executes. Every gated action carries a risk label, a countdown, a rollback line and an audit hash.

One accent, one meaning

Teal marks agent provenance, focus and links. It never signals success or severity — green, amber and red do that, always paired with a word or glyph.

Nothing hides on failure

Missing evidence, budget exhaustion, unavailable models and rejected approvals all render in place with a plain-language reason and a next step. No silent degradation.
